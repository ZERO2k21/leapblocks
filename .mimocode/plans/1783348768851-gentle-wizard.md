# Fix: Object Detector Double Topbar

## Problem
ObjectDetectorPanel renders its own `IgniteTopbar` while being inside `ProjectWorkspace` which also renders one. This creates two stacked topbars.

## Fix
**File:** `src/components/neura/ui/panels/ObjectDetectorPanel.tsx`

Remove the `IgniteTopbar` from ObjectDetectorPanel. Move the Auto toggle and Detect button into a toolbar strip at the top of the content area (below the main topbar).
